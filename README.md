## Not a search — a record

Most fintech market research starts when you ask. Cantica started
long before: it observes fintech 24/7 — funding rounds, regulatory
moves, partnerships, product launches, licensing, M&A — judges every
event, and computes original data from what it reads. A commissioned
report stands on **data no one else is holding**.

**Every number is traceable.** Each figure in your report traces back
to the source events it was computed from — with dates and links —
so your team can *verify*, not just trust. That evidence trail is the
difference between a conclusion you can act on and an opinion with
charts.

## Why the numbers can be trusted

Cantica ingests public fintech announcements — press wires,
regulators' own sites, company newsrooms — judges each event through
a versioned editorial lens, and records it in an append-only ledger.
Findings are computed from that accumulated record; every published
number is recomputable from the cited sources; published trend calls
are re-checked against every new run and scored against what actually
happened. Human judgment, AI-assisted: models propose, the math and
the editor dispose.
